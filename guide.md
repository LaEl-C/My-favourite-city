
# My Favourite City – Jos

## Introduction

Jos is my favourite city in Nigeria. It is the capital of Plateau State and I got to experience it during my NYSC days. It is a city with a unique atmosphere and natural beauty. It is a place I admire because of its refreshing environment and beautiful surroundings.

## Why I Love Jos

There are several reasons why Jos is special to me:

- **Beautiful and clean Weather:** As reluctant as I was to go there, the first thing I noticed was how clean the air was, it made me wonder what I had been breathing in Lagos. The air is so clean. Though it took a while for me to adjust to the cold, it was still nice for me to experience ice falling and the likes.
- **Natural Beauty:** The city is surrounded by hills, rocks, valleys, and beautiful landscapes. Most of the attractions are natural and so breathtaking.
- **Peaceful Environment:** Jos has a refreshing and serene atmosphere that makes it a lovely place to live and visit.
- **Cultural Diversity:** One other thing I noticed was how there were people of many cultures there. Maybe it's because different people came to serve but then outside of the camp, there were different tribes everywhere.
- **Tourist Attractions:** There are several interesting places to explore in and around Jos for example, the ten commandments.

## Interesting Places in Jos

Some of the interesting places in Jos and its surroundings include:

1. **Shere Hills:** A beautiful range of hills that attracts hikers and nature lovers.
2. **Jos Wildlife Park:** A popular tourist attraction where visitors can experience wildlife and nature.
3. **National Museum Jos:** A museum that showcases Nigerian history, culture, and traditional architecture.
4. **Assop Falls:** A beautiful waterfall located along the Jos-Abuja road.
5. **Solomon Lar Amusement Park:** A recreational destination for relaxation and entertainment.
6. **The Ten Commandments:** A popular religious landmark in Jos, Plateau State. I first went there for a program and honestly I was blown away. It is also a place where visitors can appreciate the importance of faith and biblical teachings.

## My Favourite Things About the City

I think my favourite thing about the city was the fact that there was no rush, no traffic, the people are genuinely nice. I loved retreating by myself to the mountains, those were my favourite times and then sometimes I went with friends.
